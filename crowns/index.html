👑 Crowns

A calm daily logic puzzle for Android.

How to play

Place exactly one crown in every row, every column and every colored region. Two crowns can never touch, not even diagonally. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

Features

Endless levels from 5×5 to 10×10, a new daily puzzle with streaks, hints, undo and best times. Works fully offline with no account.
