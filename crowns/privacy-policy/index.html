Privacy Policy for Crowns

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Crowns ("the app") is a puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Information the app stores

Your game progress (current level, solved puzzles, best times, daily streak and settings) is stored only on your device. The app has no user accounts and no servers of its own, and the developer does not collect or receive this information.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and information about ad interactions to serve, measure and personalize ads. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown, and can change their choice at any time from the "Privacy" button on the app's home screen.

Learn more in Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

You can delete all data stored by the app by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. You can reset your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version is always available at this address.

Contact
